Aloud Offline neural reader

Step 1 of 5

Anything you can read, read aloud.

Aloud finds the article on the page, speaks it with a neural voice that runs inside your browser, and highlights each sentence as it goes. The voice is downloaded once; after that it works with the network switched off.

Nothing leaves this device No account, no upload, no telemetry. The text never travels.
Free, and staying that way GPLv3, built on eSpeak-NG, ONNX Runtime, Piper and Kokoro.
Works on what you already read Articles, documentation, your own selection — and PDFs.

This is Aloud. It reads the page you are on with a voice that runs entirely on your own computer, and it follows along, sentence by sentence, so you never lose your place. Press the button and hear it for yourself.

The first voice downloads once — you will see its progress in the bar that appears.

Step 2 of 5

Four ways to start reading.

  • Click the Aloud button in the toolbar on any article. Click it again to pause, and again to carry on.
  • AltShiftR Press the shortcut — the same thing, without leaving the keyboard.
  • Right-click Select some text and choose “Read selection aloud”, or right-click anywhere and choose “Read from here” to start at that paragraph rather than the top.
  • PDF Open a PDF and click the button. Aloud reopens it in a reader of its own, because a PDF in a browser tab has no text an extension can reach.

A page Aloud cannot read — a browser settings page, a blank tab, an add-on gallery — shows n/a on the toolbar icon for a moment instead of failing silently.

Step 3 of 5

The bar at the bottom is the whole interface.

It appears over the page you are reading and never moves: whatever Aloud has to say — loading a voice, buffering, an error — is said in a pill above it, so a button is never anywhere but where you last saw it.

Step 4 of 5

Pick a voice that suits your machine.

Every voice runs locally, so the honest trade is speed against warmth. Aloud has measured what this browser gives it and ordered them accordingly.

Voices download once and are stored on this device — the settings page lists what is installed, previews any voice before you commit to it, and deletes what you no longer want.

Step 5 of 5

Then make it yours.

Light, dark, or whatever the system says The switch at the top of this page sets it everywhere Aloud draws — this page, the settings, the PDF reader and the bar itself.
Following the reader Keep the sentence visible, snap it to the middle of the screen, or never scroll at all and use the bar's ↧ button when you want to catch up.
Pauses and pronunciation Add a breath between sentences or sections, say “link” instead of reading a URL aloud, and expand abbreviations like “e.g.”.
The highlight Any colour you like, or off entirely if you would rather just listen.

You can reopen this walkthrough at any time from the bottom of the settings page.
