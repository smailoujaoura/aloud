The lighthouse keeper's log

Every evening at dusk, the keeper climbed the spiral stairs to light the lamp. He wrote the weather in a ledger that nobody else would ever read. Wind from the west, visibility good, two boats heading north. The entries are short, but together they make a kind of diary of the sea.
