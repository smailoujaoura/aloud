Privacy policy

Aloud collects nothing.

In effect from 15 October 2026, for Aloud 1.0 on Firefox, Firefox for Android and Chromium browsers.

There is no Aloud server, account, analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting. What you read, and everything you keep in Aloud, stays in your browser on your device. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to the developer, because nothing is collected.

What Aloud keeps on your device

Aloud stores these in your browser's extension storage, on your device only:

  • Your settings, keyboard shortcuts and colours
  • Highlights and notes you make, with their tags and the address of the page they belong to
  • Your reading queue, including the text of articles you saved to read later
  • Your reading history and the sentence you stopped at in each article or PDF
  • The video speed you chose on each site, if you use the video speed control
  • Marks you make on PDFs
  • Listening statistics: how long you listened and how many words, by day, site and language
  • Which voice reads each language, which languages you removed, and who reads every other language
  • The voice models you download

They stay until you delete them. Aloud's Library and Settings let you clear your history, statistics, reading positions and site speeds, delete notes, and remove downloaded voices. Uninstalling Aloud removes all of it. You can also export everything to a backup file, which stays wherever you save it.

When Aloud uses the network

Only in these cases, and only when you start them:

  • Downloading a voice. Voice models, and the short preview clips of voices you have not downloaded yet, come from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its download servers). Like any website, Hugging Face receives the technical details of the request, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Aloud sends it nothing about you or what you read.
  • Fetching a page you asked for. When you add a link to your reading queue, or open a PDF from the web, Aloud downloads it directly from that site, as your browser would.
  • Images in saved articles. A saved article's images, audio and video stay unloaded until you press Load images, and are then loaded from the site they came from.

Speech is made on your device. The text being read is never sent anywhere.

Access to websites

Aloud runs on a page only when you start it there: with its button, a keyboard shortcut or the right-click menu. Or on sites you add to a list in Settings, each of which your browser asks you to approve. It does not run on every site by default.

Children

Aloud collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. A change that collects anything would need your agreement first, in Aloud itself.
