Feedback

Found a bug? Have an idea?

Aloud is made by one person, and every report gets read. Tell me what went wrong, what you'd love it to do, or just how you use it.

Report a bug

Say what you were reading, what you expected, and what happened instead. The page address and a screenshot help a lot.

Open a bug report

On GitHub, with your browser and Aloud's version already filled in. You need a free GitHub account.

Ideas, questions, anything else

A voice you'd like, a site Aloud reads badly, a feature you miss, or a thank-you. All welcome.

Start a discussion

Rather not use GitHub? Get in touch on souj.dev.
